# Resume Design System

The default is an experienced-hire document with strong typography, restrained color, and no decorative graphics.

## Page

- A4 portrait.
- Margins: approximately 13 mm top/bottom and 16 mm left/right.
- Use one primary font family throughout. Japanese: Yu Gothic; Chinese: Microsoft YaHei or Noto Sans CJK SC; English: Aptos, Calibri, or Arial.
- Body text around 9 pt, metadata 8 pt, section headings 11–12 pt, name 18 pt, title 24 pt.
- Keep line spacing around 1.1–1.2 and avoid dense paragraphs.

## Color tokens

- Navy: `#17324D` for the title, names, and section headings.
- Blue: `#2D5F7A` for dates and supporting emphasis.
- Teal: `#2A8C82` for small accents and bullet markers.
- Ink: `#24313A` for body text.
- Muted: `#5E6B73` for metadata.
- Pale blue: `#EDF3F7` for section bands.
- Pale gray: `#F5F7F8` for metadata and qualification rows.
- Divider: `#CBD7DE`.

## Composition

- Put the date in the top-right corner and the document title at the upper left.
- Use a name block with a subtle contact card. Do not add a horizontal rule directly under the title.
- Use lightly shaded section bands with a navy left edge instead of underlining each heading.
- Keep experience entries and project entries together across page breaks.
- Use a footer with name, document type, and page number.
- Avoid photos, logos, flags, skill stars, progress bars, charts, and decorative icons unless the target employer explicitly requires them.

## Content density

Prefer a comfortable two- or three-page document over an unreadable one-page compression. Move supporting project detail to a later page when it improves scanning.
